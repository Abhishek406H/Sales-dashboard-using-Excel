# Sales Analysis Dashboard Project Description

• Developed an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Excel to analyze business performance and sales trends.

• Imported and organized raw sales data from multiple datasets for accurate reporting and analysis.

• Performed data cleaning and preprocessing using Excel functions to remove duplicates, handle missing values, and standardize data.

• Created Pivot Tables to summarize sales, profit, quantity, and regional performance efficiently.

• Designed dynamic Pivot Charts for better visualization of sales trends and business insights.

• Analyzed yearly sales and profit performance to identify growth patterns and business improvement opportunities.

• Conducted region-wise sales analysis to compare the performance of East, West, Central, and South regions.

• Performed state-wise sales analysis to identify top-performing and low-performing states.

• Categorized sales data by product segments such as Office Supplies, Furniture, and Technology for detailed product analysis.

• Used slicers and filters to create an interactive dashboard experience for easy data exploration.

• Monitored key performance indicators (KPIs) including Total Sales, Total Profit, and Regional Contribution.

• Identified high-revenue products and profitable business areas through data-driven analysis.

• Improved data visualization by using charts, formatting, conditional formatting, and dashboard design techniques.

• Generated meaningful business insights to support decision-making and strategic planning.

• Demonstrated strong skills in Microsoft Excel, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization, Dashboard Development, and Business Analysis.

• Enhanced analytical thinking and reporting skills by transforming raw data into actionable insights.

• Data Cleaning
• Dashboard Design
• Data Visualization
• Business Analytics
• KPI Reporting
• Sales Trend Analysis
• Interactive Slicers & Filters
